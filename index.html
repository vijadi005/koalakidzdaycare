About Us

A Home Away from Home in the Heart of Vaughan

Located in Vaughan, Koala Kidz has been a warm, safe, and nurturing childcare centre for over five years. As a proud EYES Childcare franchise, we follow a Montessori-inspired approach led by certified educators who foster independence, creativity, and confidence — preparing children for kindergarten and beyond.

Our Programs

Learning That Grows With Your Child

Every stage of early childhood matters. Our age-tailored programs support development, curiosity, and a love of learning.

🧸
Toddler Program
18 months – 2.5 years

A nurturing environment where toddlers explore, express, and build confidence. Our dedicated team supports growth through play and discovery.

  • Sensory play experiences
  • Language & communication
  • Social-emotional support
  • Outdoor time daily
🔬
Preschool Program
2.5 years – 4 years

Montessori-based learning that ignites curiosity. Children explore at their own pace, nurturing independent thinking and a love for learning.

  • Montessori materials & activities
  • Early literacy & numeracy
  • Creative arts & music
  • Structured group activities
🎓
Kindergarten Prep
4 years – 6 years

A strong foundation for your child's future — emphasizing academics, independence, and social skills to prepare them for school and lifelong success.

  • Pre-reading & writing skills
  • STEM exploration
  • Social skills & teamwork
  • School readiness focus
